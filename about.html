Our Story

Smart Marketing & Events is a sign board and branding company based in Velachery, Chennai. Our founder, Praveen Devaraj, began in 2010 as a freelance merchandising agent with V5 Global Services (PPMS) And Communic marketing solution , working on events until 2013. That included the Microsoft Windows launch and the iPhone 5S launch.

In 2017 he started his own printing unit with two partners. The partnership did not work out, and he came out of it. After working as a freelancer again from 2018, he started Smart Marketing & Events in 2022 with zero investment and two team members beside him.

We began with service only, providing manpower, and used the payments from that work to build our own printing unit. Then came a big task from Ashok Leyland, the 100 ft signage at Phoenix Market City that others refused, and the front elevation and Dhoni sticker for the new Cauvery Hospital branch in Kovilambakkam. Soon the hard jobs in Chennai started coming to us.

Today we handle signage, branding, printing, fabrication and installation. One team looks after the project from design and printing through fabrication and installation, so our clients deal with one point of contact instead of many. We work with businesses, shops, offices, brands, events and commercial spaces across Chennai.

Our office is small, but our milestones are big.
