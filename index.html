Who we are

Smart Marketing & Events is a Chennai sign board, large format printing and branding company based in Velachery. Our founder, Praveen Devaraj, began in 2010 as a freelance merchandising agent with V5 Global Services, working on events, including the Microsoft Windows launch and the iPhone 5S launch.

We started Smart with zero investment, offering manpower services first, and used those earnings to build our own printing unit. Then came Ashok Leyland, the 100 ft signage at Phoenix Market City that others refused, and the Cauvery Hospital branch opening. Today the toughest jobs in Chennai come to us.

  • More than a decade of events and branding experience
  • Design, printing, fabrication and installation under one roof
  • A small office with big milestones

Which board is right for you?

ACP board, glow sign board or LED sign board

ACP gives a clean, flat finish for your shop name. Add glow or LED boards when you want the front to stand out in the street.

LED sign board or front light and back light

Lit boards keep your brand visible after dark. Back light suits letters and logos. Front light suits large printed faces.

One way vision, vinyl sticker or poster film

Cover glass walls and windows with your brand. One way vision lets people inside see out, and vinyl is ideal for logos and offers.

Fabric, flex and framing work

Fabric prints give backdrops and stands a smooth finish. Flex suits large, budget-friendly hoardings. Framing and fabrication keep them standing strong.

Questions we are often asked

How long does a sign board take?

Most boards are ready in a few days after the design is approved. Large jobs need more time, and we confirm the date when we quote.

Do you install the boards too?

Yes. Our team installs on site, including high walls and building elevations.

Can you work from my own design?

Yes. Send your file, or we can design it for you.

Do you take work outside Chennai?

Yes, we can supply and install for projects beyond Chennai. Call us to discuss your location.
